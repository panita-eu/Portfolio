01

BMS · Transaction Redesign

2025
Primary case study·Flow & visual redesign

A brief from the Managing Director to simplify — visually and functionally — the core transaction flow used across BMS client deployments.

Brief

The MD's ask arrived at business-level: improve the visual design, tighten the UX, and shorten the flow. No formal spec, no user research attached — translating that into concrete design decisions was the actual work.

Approach

Audited the existing flow step by step to separate genuine business logic from steps that existed by habit. Rebuilt the visual hierarchy so the transaction's critical fields surface first, and reduced the number of screens a user has to move through to complete an entry.

Constraints

BMS is a TMES product, not a one-off build — this base design ships to every client, who then re-skin it to their own CI. Any change had to hold up as the foundation every deployment builds from, ruling out a full rebuild in favor of a targeted redesign.

Outcome

Across all three modules: the SKU list went from an empty placeholder to a searchable, paginated table; entries could finally be saved as drafts mid-flow; and financial fields were grouped so only what's relevant shows by default.

02

SBCS · Accounting System

2026
Supporting case study·Extending BMS for a full accounting suite

A full accounting system for a client replacing a ten-year-old one, built on the BMS component set, with the accounting-specific parts designed new.

Brief

A client, SBCS, wanted to replace an accounting system they had run for about ten years. TMES also wanted a full accounting suite inside BMS, so the design had to serve SBCS now and fit BMS later. Requirements came from the client; the design work was mine alone.

Approach

Met with the MD, PM and developers to decide what could reuse BMS patterns and what needed new design. Kept the BMS form skeleton for every document, and designed new components only where accounting needed them, such as a journal entry preview, VAT and WHT per line, and an asset disposal flow.

Constraints

I only saw the parts of the old system the client chose to show, and their screenshots were limited by consent. Neither I nor the team had deep accounting expertise, so I researched the domain myself to fill the gaps.

Outcome

Designs delivered for seven document flows across Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable and Fixed Asset, all on the shared BMS skeleton. They have since been revised by others. No measured results or client feedback yet.

03

DoiTung · Member Portal

2026
Supporting case study·AI-assisted production

A responsive membership portal for a foundation client, produced with AI from TMES's existing CRM and re-skinned to the client's own CI.

Brief

DoiTung, a foundation and TMES client, needed a CRM with a member-facing portal. TMES already had a CRM, so the aim was to turn it into the client's product quickly.

Approach

I directed Claude to generate every screen for desktop, tablet and mobile from the existing CRM, with DoiTung's CI applied. I reviewed each screen and sent corrections, following up only on selected parts to save tokens.

Constraints

The client's CI had to replace TMES's look without losing the CRM's structure, and the work had to cover three screen sizes in far less than the usual time.

Outcome

Design time fell from an estimated two to three weeks to one to two weeks. The designs are with the client for review, and there is no feedback yet.
